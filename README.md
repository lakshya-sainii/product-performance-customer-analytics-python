# Product Performance & Customer Analytics (Python)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes product performance and customer behavior using the Olist E-Commerce dataset. The objective was to identify top-performing product categories, understand customer purchasing patterns, evaluate customer satisfaction through review ratings, and measure the impact of delivery performance on business performance.

Python was used for data cleaning, transformation, exploratory data analysis, and visualization. The analysis focused on revenue generation, product demand, pricing trends, customer reviews, and delivery efficiency to uncover actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- SQL
- Olist E-Commerce Dataset

## Key Business Questions

- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories sell the highest quantity of products?
- Which categories have the highest average product prices?
- Which categories receive the lowest customer ratings?
- How does delivery performance impact customer satisfaction?
- Which product categories are both profitable and efficient?
- What business actions can improve customer experience and sales performance?

## Key Insights

- Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- Bed Bath Table recorded the highest quantity of products sold.
- Computers had the highest average product prices.
- Security & Services received the lowest average customer ratings.
- Office Furniture showed lower customer satisfaction due to delivery-related issues.
- High-revenue categories generally maintained better customer ratings and stronger sales performance.
- Delivery efficiency played a significant role in customer satisfaction.

## Business Recommendations

- Improve logistics performance for low-rated categories.
- Prioritize inventory planning for high-demand products.
- Focus marketing efforts on top-performing categories.
- Investigate customer complaints in low-rated segments.
- Enhance delivery operations to improve customer retention.

## Outcome

The analysis provides valuable insights into product performance, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency. These findings can help businesses optimize product strategies, improve customer experience, increase profitability, and support data-driven decision-making.
